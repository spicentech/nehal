Pin 02 · About

Signal & verse.

I'm Nehal Agarwal — a first-year B.Tech student in Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE) at IIIT Allahabad. I cleared my Class 12 boards and JEE Mains, and landed here chasing a mix of hard engineering and quiet creative discipline.

Outside the classroom, I run SPICEN TECHNOLOGIES, code websites and tools, and write shayari and poetry in Hindi and Urdu — two very different languages that somehow share the same head.

I'm generally a chill, happy person who's happiest outdoors, over a chessboard, or on a badminton court.
